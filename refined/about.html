About Ecooza

Established 2024
Our Story

Building a Greener Future Together

Ecooza was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to make sustainable, eco-friendly products accessible to businesses across India. We recognized a growing need among hotels, restaurants, corporate offices, and event organizers for high-quality, environmentally responsible alternatives to conventional products.

What started as a passion project has evolved into a comprehensive B2B marketplace, connecting conscious businesses with carefully curated eco-friendly products. From biodegradable cutlery to sustainable stationery, every item we offer is selected with the planet's wellbeing in mind.

Today, we proudly serve hundreds of businesses across India, helping them reduce their environmental footprint while maintaining the quality and professionalism their customers expect. Our white-label solutions allow businesses to showcase their commitment to sustainability with their own branding.

Our Mission

To empower businesses across India with access to high-quality, eco-friendly products that help them operate sustainably without compromising on quality or professionalism. We strive to make green choices the easy choice for every business.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted B2B platform for sustainable products, creating a future where every business transaction contributes positively to environmental preservation. We envision a world where eco-friendly is the norm, not the exception.

Our Values

Sustainability, Quality, Transparency, and Innovation drive everything we do. We believe in honest partnerships, rigorous product vetting, and continuous improvement to serve our customers and the planet better every day.
